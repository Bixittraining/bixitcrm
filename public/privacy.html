Privacy Policy

BIX Academy · Last updated: 25 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BIX Academy ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information through our CRM system, including information received via integrations with Meta (Facebook/Instagram Lead Ads, WhatsApp Business), Google Ads, and JustDial.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details submitted through lead forms: name, phone number, email address, and course interest.
  • Messages sent to our WhatsApp Business number, for the purpose of responding to enquiries.
  • Enrollment, payment, and follow-up records for students and prospective students who engage with BIX Academy.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to enquiries about our courses and programs.
  • To manage enrollment, scheduling, and fee payment for students.
  • To send relevant follow-up communication about courses a person has expressed interest in.

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

Data from Meta Platforms

When someone submits a Lead Ad form on our Facebook or Instagram Page, or messages our WhatsApp Business number, that information is delivered to our CRM via Meta's official APIs and webhooks, and used solely for the purposes described above.

Data Retention

We retain lead and student information for as long as necessary to provide our services and to comply with applicable record-keeping requirements.
